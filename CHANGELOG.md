# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-02

First public release.

- **Library** (`engstoch`, 20 modules, NumPy only): special functions, pseudo-random generators and their tests,
  variate generation, Monte Carlo and variance reduction, quasi-Monte Carlo, discrete-time Markov chains, random
  walks and branching processes, Poisson and renewal processes, continuous-time chains and reaction networks,
  queueing formulas and simulation, a discrete-event simulation engine with three models, output analysis,
  Brownian motion, SDE solvers with multilevel Monte Carlo, Gaussian processes, time series and MCMC, example
  models and four course data files.
- **Course**: 20 executed notebooks with learning objectives, "Inside the algorithm" sections and exercises, and
  20 notebooks of worked solutions (60 exercises), ending in a call-centre staffing project from logged data.
- **Validation**: 306 checks against SciPy, closed-form results, published reference values and the theory,
  run with warnings as errors.
